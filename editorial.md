## Strongest objections and decisions

- Chentsov-style uniqueness is conditional on a statistical invariance
  requirement across models. The choice to represent prognosis as a probability
  distribution, to value its coordinates, and to measure effort this way remains
  substantive. Other geometries can encode relevant costs and dynamics.
- Points p, q and r are distributions, not three people. A Riemannian triangle
  inequality does not determine whether one person may delegate an obligation.
  A detour can also be longer in a flat geometry. Curvature does not prove that
  concern cannot be composed through institutions.
- The conformal tensor factor f is a squared length multiplier; path effort
  scales by sqrt(f). The present code measures a fixed Fisher geodesic under
  another metric, not necessarily that metric's shortest path. Different
  carers' costs do not make each individual Riemannian metric asymmetric.
- The kin/friend/stranger labels merely name assigned efficiency values. Remove
  the empirical interpretation and report an explicit sensitivity calculation.
- Outcome entropy is neither a menu of actions nor recipient authority. A
  predictable flourishing outcome can be freely chosen; an imposed lottery can
  have high entropy. Keep the calculation as an entropy-floor utility trade-off
  and implement permission, refusal, changed goals and revocation separately.
- The code uses weights (0, 0.5, 1), while the prose defines viability as coping
  plus flourishing probability. Call the former an assigned utility and state
  its weights. Uniformity is a chosen comparison, not a model of abandonment.
- Canguilhem's ability to revise norms is not equivalent to Shannon entropy.
  Neither a vertex nor coordinate singularity establishes coercion or pathology.
- Coupling directed toward harm differs observably from help. The missile
  example cannot be said to score well on an objective of increasing the
  recipient's viability. Phenomenological limits do not erase this distinction.

## Numerical verification decisions

The old curvature report rounds a nonzero residual to 0.0; a direct check at
(0.12, 0.12) differs from one quarter by about 3.1e-7. Retain full precision,
test finite-difference sensitivity, and compare conformal curvature against an
independently derived analytic formula. Compute spherical area independently
of angle excess before reporting a Gauss-Bonnet residual. Add a flat detour
counterexample, stable entropy optimization, and executable permission tests.
These are numerical/implementation checks, not empirical validation of care.
